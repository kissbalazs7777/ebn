Privacy Policy – EBN Tools

Last updated: 22 November 2026

EBN Tools ("the app") is a private software tool operated by the creator of the YouTube channel Europe By Numbers ("we"). It is used by a single person to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Analytics and metadata of our own YouTube channel and videos (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers, traffic sources, video titles and descriptions).
  • Public comments on our own videos, so that we can read and reply to them.
  • Public statistics of other public YouTube channels and videos (for example subscriber and view counts), for content research.
  • When enabled: uploading our own videos, thumbnails and captions to our own channel.

The app does not access any other person's private data, and it does not use YouTube data for advertising.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to plan, produce and evaluate videos for our own channel.
  • Data is stored locally on the operator's computer. It is not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • The OAuth access token is stored locally and is used only to call the YouTube API on behalf of our own account.
  • Stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Cookies

The app is a local program and does not place cookies on any device. This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/connections (see also security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). After revocation the app deletes the stored token and any data obtained with it.
